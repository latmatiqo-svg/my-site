About Artidian

European manufacturer based in Athens, Greece

Based in Athens, Greece, our company designs and manufactures machines for stretch ceiling production with a harpoon mounting system — specifically for welding PVC film with a harpoon.

Our equipment is suitable for industrial-scale ceiling production: from start-up workshops to expanding existing factories across Europe, the Middle East and beyond.
